What We Do

Community. Culture. Connection.

From tea gatherings to soccer matches, we create spaces where our community can come together, share stories, and feel at home. All events are free and open to everyone.

☕

Tea Socials

Monthly gatherings featuring traditional Sudanese tea (شاي), conversation, and connection. Potluck-style and always welcoming.

⚽

Sports & Recreation

Soccer days, pickup games, and family sports events. Staying active together while building community bonds.

🕌

Cultural Celebrations

Eid gatherings, Sudanese Independence Day (January 1), and cultural events that honor our heritage and traditions.

📣

Advocacy & Awareness

Raising awareness about the humanitarian situation in Sudan through social media, panels, and partnerships with advocacy organizations.

🤝

Mutual Support

Connecting newly arrived Sudanese families with resources, community members, and one another in metro Atlanta.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Family Days

Kid-friendly events with activities for children and parents alike—because community means everyone is welcome.

Our Story

From Emory to All of Atlanta

Atlanta For Sudan started as a student organization at Emory University—a group of Sudanese students brought together by a shared homeland, shared culture, and a shared determination to keep Sudan's story alive.

As our community grew beyond campus, we realized our work was bigger than any one university. We transitioned to a community-wide organization, committed to serving every Sudanese person in metro Atlanta.

"A connected diaspora is a powerful force for both local belonging and lasting change back home."
