Who We Are

From a student organization at Emory to a community for all Sudanese people in Atlanta — this is our story.

Our Story

From Emory to All of Atlanta

Atlanta For Sudan was born on the campus of Emory University, where a group of Sudanese students found one another at a time when our homeland was in crisis. We were brought together not just by shared nationality, but by shared love — for our families back home, for our culture, and for each other.

Under the name Emory4Sudan, we organized events, raised awareness, and grew a social media community of over 1,000 followers. But we quickly realized something important: the Sudanese community in Atlanta is far bigger than any one campus.

In 2024, we made the transition from university club to community organization, renaming ourselves Atlanta For Sudan — a name that reflects our expanded vision and our commitment to every Sudanese person in metro Atlanta, whether you arrived last week or were born here.

Mission & Vision

Why We Do This Work

Our Mission

Atlanta For Sudan unites the Sudanese community in Atlanta through cultural connection, mutual support, and collective action for our homeland.

Our Vision

A thriving, connected Sudanese community in Atlanta that preserves our culture, supports one another, and amplifies the voices of those still in Sudan.

Extended Mission

Atlanta For Sudan exists to create a home away from home for Sudanese people in the Atlanta metropolitan area. We bring our community together through cultural gatherings, awareness campaigns, and mutual support—honoring our heritage while advocating for peace and justice in Sudan.

Core Values

What We Stand For

These values guide everything we do — from how we plan events to how we treat one another.

🤝

Ubuntu / التكافل (Al-Takaful)

Community interdependence — we rise together. No one in our community is left behind, and every person's success strengthens us all.

🌟

Cultural Pride

Celebrating Sudanese heritage, food, music, language, and traditions. We honor who we are and where we come from.

✊

Advocacy with Dignity

Raising awareness about Sudan without sensationalism. We center Sudanese voices and frame our people's resilience and agency, not just suffering.

🏠

Radical Hospitality

Every Sudanese person in Atlanta has a place with us. Whether you arrived last week or were born in Georgia, you are family.

💬

Transparency

We are open about our work, our finances, and our decision-making. Our community deserves to know how we operate.

About Sudan

Why This Work Matters

Since April 2023, Sudan has been engulfed in a devastating civil conflict between the Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF) and the Rapid Support Forces (RSF). The crisis has displaced over 10 million people, collapsed healthcare systems, and created one of the world's worst humanitarian emergencies.

For the Sudanese diaspora in Atlanta — and across the world — this is not an abstract news story. It is family members, neighbors, and home.

Atlanta For Sudan believes that a connected diaspora is one of the most powerful forces for change. By building community here, we strengthen our ability to support those back home and keep Sudan's story alive.

Key Context (as of 2025–2026):
  • Civil war began April 2023
  • 10+ million internally displaced
  • Severe famine and healthcare collapse
  • Diaspora communities are critical for support and advocacy

We focus on Sudanese resilience and agency, not victimhood. We amplify Sudanese voices, provide context, and always include ways to take action.
