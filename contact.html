Organizations & Partners

Are You an Organization?

We're actively seeking partnerships with organizations that serve Atlanta's refugee and immigrant communities, advocacy groups, and cultural institutions.

🤝

Community Partners

If you serve Sudanese or broader refugee and immigrant communities in Atlanta, we'd love to explore collaboration, referrals, and co-events.

Examples: resettlement agencies, community centers, legal aid, healthcare providers.

📣

Advocacy Organizations

We partner with organizations working on Sudan awareness and diaspora advocacy at the local, national, and international level.

Examples: Sudan Uprising, SACDO, humanitarian organizations.

🏪

Local Businesses

Sudanese and African-owned businesses in Atlanta — let's support each other. We promote community businesses and welcome venue and in-kind partnerships.

Examples: Nanna's Sudanese Cuisine and other community businesses.

Where We Are

Metro Atlanta, Georgia

Atlanta For Sudan operates across the greater Atlanta metropolitan area, with particular focus on communities where Sudanese people live and work.

Primary Areas

  • City of Atlanta
  • DeKalb County (especially Clarkston)
  • Gwinnett County

Also Serving

  • Fulton County
  • Cobb County
  • Clayton County

Events are held at public parks, community spaces, and partner venues throughout the metro area. All event locations are shared in our WhatsApp group closer to the event date.
