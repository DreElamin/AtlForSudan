Support Us

Support Our Work

Atlanta For Sudan is a volunteer-driven, community-funded organization. Right now, our events are kept low-cost through the potluck model and volunteer labor. As we grow, small donations help us cover:

  • Event supplies (cups, napkins, plates for tea socials)
  • Promotional materials and printing
  • Venue deposits for larger events
  • Tech tools (website hosting, design)
  • Emergency support for community members in need
Note: We are currently an informal community organization (not a 501(c)(3) nonprofit). Donations are not tax-deductible at this time. We are exploring formal structures for the future.

For now, the most powerful thing you can do is show up, spread the word, and bring a friend to our events.
