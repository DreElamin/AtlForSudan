Regular Programming

What We Host

We rotate through different event types to find what resonates with our community. Here's what you can expect from Atlanta For Sudan throughout the year.

☕

Monthly Tea Socials

Our flagship event — informal gatherings featuring traditional Sudanese tea (شاي), potluck food, and open conversation. Usually at local parks.

Frequency: Monthly or bi-monthly

⚽

Soccer & Sports

Pickup soccer games and sports days at Atlanta area parks. All skill levels welcome — this is about community, not competition.

Frequency: Monthly

🌙

Holiday Celebrations

Eid celebrations, Sudanese Independence Day (January 1), and other cultural and religious observances that bring our community together.

Frequency: Seasonal

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Family Days

Larger outdoor events with activities for children and parents. Think community picnics, games, and group activities for all generations.

Frequency: Quarterly

💼

Professional Meetups

Networking and connection events for Sudanese young professionals, students, and entrepreneurs in the Atlanta area.

Frequency: Quarterly

📚

Awareness & Education

Panels, film screenings, and speaker events about Sudan — for our community and for allies who want to learn and take action.

Frequency: As opportunities arise

Get Involved

Host an Event

Our events work because of community members like you. If you have a space, a skill, or an idea for bringing people together, we'd love to hear from you.

Have a backyard or apartment with a clubhouse? Know how to cook Sudanese food for a crowd? Want to organize a sports day? You can make it happen.

  • Host a small tea gathering at your home
  • Organize a pickup game at your local park
  • Cook for a community event
  • Help with photos and social media documentation
  • Suggest a new event idea

Event Guidelines

  • ✅ All events are free to attend
  • ✅ Family-friendly by default
  • ✅ Open to all — Sudanese and allies welcome
  • ✅ Photos only with consent
  • ✅ Potluck model — bring to share, or just come
  • ✅ Inclusive of all Sudanese backgrounds and regions
